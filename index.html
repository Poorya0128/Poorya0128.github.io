Four areas of focus

Designed around moments where clarity matters.

Four practical kinds of support, organized around a nurse’s real workload.

01 / RECOGNIZE

Recognize possible disease patterns sooner.

Connect symptoms, observations, history, and recent changes to surface patterns worth reviewing and escalating.

02 / PREVENT

Catch preventable nursing errors.

Flag conflicting information, missed checks, and unfinished follow-up for review.

03 / TIME

Keep medication care visible.

Surface upcoming and overdue windows, checks, and follow-up while the care team keeps control.

04 / CONNECT

Find a better way to say it.

Prepare clear, respectful explanations and questions for patients, families, and handoffs.

One shift view

Patients and priorities, without the hunt.

PooryaAI condenses the shift into a short, reviewable view. The nurse verifies the context and decides what happens next.

01

Assigned patient list for each nurse

See every assigned patient with current status, recent changes, medication windows, and watch items.

02

Prioritized task queue

Order care tasks by urgency, timing, clinical risk, and unfinished follow-up—then let the nurse adjust the order.

03

Focused safety and disease cues

Surface a concise prompt when a possible disease pattern, conflict, or missed check needs review.

04

Communication and handoff

Prepare a plain-language explanation or structured handoff that the nurse can check and refine.

Safety and clear boundaries

Support for nurses. Never a replacement.

01 / NURSE DECIDES

Support, not replacement

PooryaAI does not diagnose, prescribe, or administer medication. Assessment, decisions, and escalation stay with the nurse and the clinical team.

02 / SHOW THE REASONING

Prompts you can check

Important prompts show the cues behind them, the uncertainty, and what still needs verification.

03 / VALIDATE FIRST

No patient care yet

Clinical use would come only after co-design with nurses, privacy review, and careful evaluation of missed or unsafe cues.

Questions, answered

What PooryaAI is—and is not.

The essentials about scope, safety, and current availability.

Who is PooryaAI for?

Working nurses and their clinical teams — not students or exam preparation.

Does it diagnose or give medication?

No. It flags patterns and timing cues for a nurse to review. See Safety and clear boundaries above.

How is patient information handled?

Do not send us patient information. This is a concept preview — see Privacy below.

Can I use it in patient care today?

No. It is in development. Pilots with nurses come first.

Privacy

PooryaAI is a concept preview. Do not send patient information to us — in email or anywhere else.

Emails to contact@pooryaai.me are used only to reply to you.
