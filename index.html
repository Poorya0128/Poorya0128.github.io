The reality of a shift

The plan you made at handoff is obsolete by 9 AM.

One nurse carries five or six patients at once: medications, vitals, wound care, family questions, all competing for the same hour. Interruptions arrive every few minutes.

A late or missed medication is a patient-safety event, and the fear of it follows you home. The problem was never a lack of dedication. It was a lack of clarity.

Four areas of focus

Designed around momentswhere clarity matters.

Four practical kinds of support, organized around a nurse's real workload.

01 / Recognize

Recognize patterns sooner

Connect symptoms, observations, history and recent changes to surface patterns worth reviewing and escalating, for the nurse to assess. Never an autonomous diagnosis.

02 / Prevent

Catch preventable errors

Flag conflicting information, missed checks and unfinished follow-up so the issue is reviewed before it reaches the patient.

03 / Time

Keep medication care visible

Surface upcoming and overdue windows, verification steps and follow-up cues while administration decisions stay with the care team.

04 / Connect

Find a better way to say it

Prepare clear, respectful explanations and questions for patients, families and handoffs, adapted to the moment and reviewed before use.

Safety before scale

Support for nurses.Never a replacement.

  1. 01 / Nurse decides

    Not a diagnosis engine

    PooryaAi does not diagnose, prescribe or administer. Assessment, decisions and escalation stay with the nurse and the clinical team.

  2. 02 / Show the reasoning

    Prompts you can check

    Important prompts show the cues behind them, the uncertainty, and what still needs verification before anything is acted on.

  3. 03 / Validate first

    No patient care yet

    Clinical use would come only after co-design with nurses, privacy review, and careful evaluation of missed or unsafe cues.

Questions, answered

What PooryaAi is(and isn't).

Who is PooryaAi for?

Working nurses and their clinical teams. Not students or exam preparation.

Does it diagnose or give medication?

No. It flags patterns and timing cues for a nurse to review. See Safety and clear boundaries above.

How is patient information handled?

Do not send us patient information. The pilot is a workflow study without identifiable patient information.

Can I use it in patient care today?

Not yet. The pilot is a workflow study without identifiable patient information, not clinical use.

Does the pilot cost anything?

No. The pilot is free for participating nurses.

Does PooryaAi connect to my hospital's systems?

No. PooryaAi is a personal agent for the nurse. It doesn't integrate with hospital systems, so there's nothing for IT to install.

When will PooryaAi be available?

We're in private pilot now, building it with working nurses. Join the pilot for early access.
